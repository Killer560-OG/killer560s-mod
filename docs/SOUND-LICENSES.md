# Sound licenses

The mod's own sounds are original works made for it. None of them contains sampled or third-party audio.

| File | What | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `assets/killer560smod/sounds/correction_alarm.ogg` | Correction Alarm siren, synthesized with numpy | CC0 1.0 (public domain) |
| `assets/killer560smod/sounds/click_soft.ogg`, `click_tick.ogg`, `click_pop.ogg`, `click_wood.ogg`, `click_chime.ogg`, `click_bleep.ogg` | The Click Sounds presets (Soft Tap, Tick, Pop, Wood Knock, Chime, Bleep), each a few sine and noise envelopes | CC0 1.0 (public domain) |

The click presets are generated by `tools/gen-click-sounds.py` (numpy and soundfile; mono, 44.1 kHz, 40-220 ms), so they can be
rebuilt or changed from the script. Sounds a player imports (Home > Click Sounds > Open Sound Folder) stay in their own
config folder and are never part of a release jar; their license is the player's own business.
